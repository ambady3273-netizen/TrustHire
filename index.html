FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our verification and escrow protections.

How does TrustHire verify employers and jobs? +

All employers undergo multi-step verification including government registration (GST/PAN or Aadhaar), address checks, and business email confirmation. Our AI system also inspects job descriptions for scam keywords before publication.

How does the secure escrow payment work? +

When an employer hires you, they deposit the promised compensation into a secure TrustHire Escrow vault. Once you complete the gig and both parties confirm the work, funds are instantly released to your account.

Can college students with no prior experience join? +

Yes! TrustHire is built especially for students. We have hundreds of beginner-friendly openings like event helpers, weekend cafe staff, tutoring, campus promoters, and virtual assistants.

Are there any registration fees for candidates? +

No, TrustHire is 100% free for students and job seekers. We never ask candidates to pay for applying, training, or job placements. Any employer demanding money upfront is blocked permanently.
