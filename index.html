Features

Everything built-in.
Nothing to pay for.

TrustHire gives every job seeker and employer a full set of professional tools — completely free.

🤖

AI Scam Detection v2.0

15 independent detection layers analyze every job posting before it reaches seekers. 200+ scam patterns including regional Tamil, Hindi fraud phrases.

✅

KYC Verification

Employers submit Government ID and selfie. Admin reviews within 24 hours. Verified badge shown on all their job listings.

🔒

Escrow Payments

Employer's salary is held securely before work begins. Released only after you confirm the job is done. You always get paid.

📍

GPS Live Tracking

Share your real-time location with your employer. Find jobs within 20 km using the Near Me filter with distance badges.

💬

Real-time Chat

Instant messaging between employer and accepted seekers. No phone calls needed. Chat history is saved forever.

⭐

Trust Score System

After every completed job, both parties rate each other. Your Trust Score builds up over time — like a reputation passport.

📋

Work Portfolio

Every completed job adds to your work history portfolio. Employers can view it — like a LinkedIn for blue-collar workers.

🚨

Report a Job

See something suspicious? Tap the flag icon on any job. Our admin team reviews reports within 24 hours.

⏱️

Attendance Tracking

GPS-based clock in/out system. Employer sees your attendance log with timestamps and location proof.

How It Works

Get started in 3 minutes

No interviews, no resume needed. Just download, register, and start applying.

1

Download & Register

Download the APK, install it, and register with your email or phone number. Choose your role: Job Seeker or Employer.

2

Browse AI-verified Jobs

Every job is scanned by AI before you see it. Use Near Me filter, salary filter, or search by category to find the right job.

3

Apply & Chat

Tap Apply Now. If accepted, you can chat directly with the employer. Your payment is protected by escrow from day one.

4

Complete & Get Paid

Finish the job, employer marks it complete, escrow releases your payment. Rate each other to build your Trust Score.
