Features

Everything built-in.
Nothing to pay for.

Complete professional tools for both job seekers and employers — at zero cost.

🤖

AI Scam Detection v2.0

15 independent layers analyze every job. 200+ patterns including regional Tamil, Hindi, and Kannada scam phrases. Blocked before you ever see them.

🪪

KYC Verified Employers

Every employer submits Government ID + live selfie. Admin-reviewed within 24 hours. Verified badge on all their listings. You always know they're real.

🔒

Escrow Payments

Employer's salary is locked before work starts. Released only after job completion confirmed. You always get paid for work you do.

📍

GPS Live Tracking

Share real-time location with employer. Near Me filter shows jobs within 20 km. Clock In/Out with GPS timestamp proof.

💬

Real-time Chat

Direct messaging between employer and workers. Full chat history. Read receipts. No phone numbers needed.

⭐

Trust Score System

Both sides rate each other after every job. Your score builds automatically — proof you're reliable that employers can see.

📋

Work Portfolio

Completed jobs auto-add to your portfolio with company, salary, rating. Like a LinkedIn that builds itself from real work.

🎁

Referral System

Invite friends with your unique code. Both earn +5 Trust Score on join. Higher score means more visibility to employers.

🌐

7 Indian Languages

Full UI in English, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi. Switch anytime in settings. Made for every Indian worker.
