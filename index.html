FAQ

Common questions answered

Is TrustHire completely free to use? +
Yes, TrustHire is 100% free for job seekers. Download the APK, create an account and start applying to jobs. There are no subscriptions, no hidden charges, and no premium plans needed.
How do I install the APK on my Android phone? +
Download the APK from this page. On your Android phone, go to Settings → Security → enable "Install from unknown sources" or "Install unknown apps". Then open the downloaded APK file and tap Install. The app will be ready in seconds.
How does the AI scam detection work? +
Every job posting is analyzed through 21 AI detection layers including salary plausibility checks, behavioral contradiction detection, phone number intelligence, industry risk profiling, and social engineering pattern matching. Jobs get a SAFE, LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH risk rating before you see them.
What is the Trust Score? +
Trust Score is a 0–100 rating assigned to every user. It's calculated based on KYC verification, job completion history, employer reviews, and platform behavior. A higher trust score gets you more job opportunities and faster hiring.
How does escrow payment protect me? +
When an employer hires you, the agreed payment is locked in escrow. After you complete the work and both parties confirm, the funds are released to your account. This means the employer cannot refuse to pay after work is done.
Which Android version is required? +
TrustHire requires Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher. This covers the vast majority of Android devices in India. The app is about 58 MB in size.
